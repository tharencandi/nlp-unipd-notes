Natural Language Processing

Comprehensive Course Notes
University of Padova • Mathematics Department • 2026
Welcome! These comprehensive notes cover the Natural Language Processing course offered at the University of Padova. The material synthesizes lecture content, textbook references, and additional research to provide a complete reference for students and NLP enthusiasts.

📚 Course Structure

1. The Basics

  • Elements of Linguistics
  • Learning Tasks & Data Collection
  • Text Processing

2. Representation

  • Bag-of-Words & TF-IDF
  • Distributional Semantics
  • Word Embeddings
  • Document Embeddings

3. Language Models

  • N-Gram Models
  • Neural Networks for Language
  • RNNs & LSTMs
  • Attention & Transformers
  • BERT & GPT

4. Applications

  • Parsing
  • Question Answering & RAG
  • Sentiment Analysis
  • Coreference Resolution
  • And more...

📖 Textbook References

  • Jurafsky & Martin – Speech and Language Processing (3rd ed.)
  • Jacob Eisenstein – Natural Language Processing, MIT Press (2019)
