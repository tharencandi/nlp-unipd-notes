## Course Structure

### The Basics (Chapters 1-4)
Foundational concepts including linguistic elements, learning tasks, preprocessing, and text processing techniques.

### Chapter 5: Representation
How to represent text computationally, from simple bag-of-words models to sophisticated word and document embeddings.

### Chapter 6: Language Models
Core language modelling approaches, progressing from n-grams through neural networks to modern transformer-based architectures like BERT and GPT.

### Chapter 7: Applications
Practical applications of NLP techniques including parsing, question answering, sentiment analysis, coreference resolution, and information retrieval.
